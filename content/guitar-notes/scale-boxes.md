Learning the fretboard does not require memorizing twelve distinct sets of shapes. The guitar neck is a repeating, symmetrical grid organized around **5 interlocking scale boxes** (derived from the CAGED system).

Once you visualize all 5 boxes interconnected across the neck, you unlock:
1. **The Relative Minor Connection:** Every Major scale shares the exact same notes and shapes as its **Relative Minor scale** (anchoring 3 semitones / 3 frets down on the 6th degree).
2. **Indian Sargam Integration:** Western scale intervals map directly to Indian Classical Swaras (**Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni**), uniting Western CAGED visualization with Indian melodic phrasing.
3. **Movable Transposition:** Shift the entire 5-box interconnected chain up or down the fretboard to play in any of the 12 musical keys.

---

## 1. Interval Engine & Swara Mapping

A standard Major Scale (Bilawal Thaat) and its Natural Minor Scale (Asavari Thaat) share the exact same step intervals and Swaras:

<div class="table-responsive">

| Scale Degree | Major Interval | Indian Swara (Bilawal) | Step Progression | Relative Minor Degree | Minor Swara (Asavari) |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---:|:---|:---|
| **1** | **Root (Tonic)** | **Sa (Shuddha)** | Root | 3rd Degree (Gandhar) | Ga |
| **2** | Major 2nd | **Re (Shuddha)** | + Whole (2 frets) | 4th Degree (Madhyam) | Ma |
| **3** | Major 3rd | **Ga (Shuddha)** | + Whole (2 frets) | 5th Degree (Pancham) | Pa |
| **4** | Perfect 4th | **Ma (Shuddha)** | + Half (1 fret) | 6th Degree (Dhaivat) | Dha (Komal) |
| **5** | Perfect 5th | **Pa (Shuddha)** | + Whole (2 frets) | 7th Degree (Nishad) | Ni (Komal) |
| **6** | Major 6th | **Dha (Shuddha)** | + Whole (2 frets) | **Minor Root (Sa)** | **Sa** |
| **7** | Major 7th | **Ni (Shuddha)** | + Whole (2 frets) | 2nd Degree (Rishabh) | Re |
| **8 / 1**| **Octave Root** | **Sa (Taar Saptak)** | + Half (1 fret) | 3rd Degree (Octave) | Ga |

</div>

> **The 3-Semitone Relative Shift:**
> * In **C Major**, your Major Root is **C** (`:root` / Red).
> * Its Relative Minor is **A Minor**, with the Root located on **A** (`:start` / Gold), exactly **3 frets lower**.
> * The notes and shapes are **100% identical**; only your starting point and resolution note change!
