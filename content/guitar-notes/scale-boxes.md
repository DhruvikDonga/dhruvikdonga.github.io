Learning the fretboard does not require memorizing twelve distinct fingerings for every single key. The guitar is a symmetrical instrument organized around **5 interconnected box patterns** (based on the CAGED system). 

Once you memorize these 5 shapes, you unlock two massive musical superpowers:
1. **Relative Minor for Free:** Every Major scale shares the exact same notes and shapes as its **Relative Minor scale** (starting 3 semitones / 3 frets down on the 6th degree).
2. **Indian Sargam Integration:** Every Western scale degree maps directly to Indian Classical Swaras (**Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni**), bridging Western modal theory and Indian melodic improvisation.

## 2. The Whole-Step / Half-Step Engine

Every scale in Western music is constructed using a fixed sequence of **Whole steps (W = 2 frets)** and **Half steps (H = 1 fret)**:

* **Music Notes:** `A – A# – B – C – C# – D – D# – E – F – F# – G – G#`
* **Major Scale Formula:** `W – W – H – W – W – W – H`
* **Natural Minor Scale Formula:** `W – H – W – W – H – W – W`

<div class="table-responsive">

| Scale | Starting Note (Root) | Step-by-Step Interval Formula | Resulting Notes |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **C Major** | **C** | C `(+W)` D `(+W)` E `(+H)` F `(+W)` G `(+W)` A `(+W)` B `(+H)` **C** | **C – D – E – F – G – A – B** |
| **A Minor** | **A** | A `(+W)` B `(+H)` C `(+W)` D `(+W)` E `(+H)` F `(+W)` G `(+W)` **A** | **A – B – C – D – E – F – G** |

</div>

> **The Relative Minor Relationship:**
> * **Same Physical Fretboard Shape:** The notes are 100% identical.
> * **C Major (Bilawal Thaat):** Starts and resolves on the **red note (`C:root`)**.
> * **A Minor (Asavari Thaat):** Starts and resolves on the **gold note (`A:start`)**, exactly 3 frets / 3 semitones down from C.
> * Each C Major CAGED box corresponds directly to an A Minor Box number in the natural minor system!

### Practice Protocol:
1. **Watch the Start & Stop badges:** The pulse begins at **Start** and cycles up to the **Stop** resolution target.
2. **Sing the Sargam:** 
   * When playing **C Major**, treat **C** as `Sa` (`Sa - Re - Ga - Ma - Pa - Dha - Ni - Sa`).
   * When playing **A Minor**, treat **A** as `Sa` (`Sa - Re - Ga(k) - Ma - Pa - Dha(k) - Ni(k) - Sa`).
3. **Use the Speed Multiplier:** Slow the playback down to `0.5x` while learning finger transitions, then practice along at `1.0x` and `1.5x`.
