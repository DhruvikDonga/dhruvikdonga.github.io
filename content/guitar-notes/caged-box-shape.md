The **CAGED system** is the foundational visual framework for organizing the guitar neck. By linking the five basic open chord forms (**C – A – G – E – D**) with their corresponding scale fingerings, you can navigate the entire fretboard in any key without getting lost.

Every CAGED major scale box contains its relative natural minor scale starting on the 6th degree (**A**, located 3 semitones / 3 frets lower), mapped directly to Indian Classical Sargam swaras (**Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni**).

### Practice Steps
1. **Trace the Root Shapes:** Before playing the full scale, locate the root notes (**Red for C**, **Gold for A**) and visualize the chord shape that frames them.
2. **Sing the Sargam:** Vocally intone `Sa - Re - Ga - Ma - Pa - Dha - Ni - Sa` to train your ear alongside finger placement.
3. **Shift Across Positions:** Practice ascending in **Box 1 (C-Shape)**, shifting into **Box 2 (A-Shape)** on the higher strings, and resolving into **Box 4 (E-Shape)** at the 8th fret.
