## 🎸 Essential Open Chords

Open chords anchor guitar harmony by utilizing open strings alongside fretted notes within the first 3 frets.

### 1. C Major
The classic C shape built from the tonic note **C** on the 5th string:

* **Strings Played:** 5 (A), 4 (D), 3 (G - open), 2 (B), 1 (high E - open)
* **Root Note:** C (5th string, 3rd fret)

## 🎼 F Major Scale Design

The **F Major Scale** consists of seven natural pitches with one flat ($B\flat$):
$$\text{F} - \text{G} - \text{A} - \text{B}\flat - \text{C} - \text{D} - \text{E}$$

Because the tonic root starts on the 1st fret of the low E string, playing this box exercises finger independence across the 1st to 3rd frets.

| String | Fret 1 | Fret 2 | Fret 3 |

| **4 (D)** | — | **E** | **F** *(Root)* |
| **5 (A)** | **B♭** | — | **C** |
| **6 (Low E)** | **F** *(Root)* | — | **G** |

### Practice Tips
* **Fretting Hand:** Keep your index finger dedicated to fret 1, middle finger to fret 2, and ring/pinky to fret 3.
* **Tonic Landmarks:** Target the three octave roots (**6:1**, **4:3**, and **1:1**) to build fretboard awareness.
